This Privacy Policy explains how The Cobraverse Company LLC ("Cobraverse," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit cobraverse.net, lasercobras.com, and our related sites and services (the "Services"), including our mobile game Laser Cobras! Assault and our Etsy shop. Purchases made through Etsy are additionally governed by Etsy's own privacy policy, which applies to the marketplace itself — see Section 3. By using the Services, you agree to this Policy and to our Terms of Use.

1. Who we are

The Cobraverse Company LLC is a small media studio based in Colorado, United States, that produces video games, videos, and merchandise. You can contact us any time through our contact form.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide so we can respond to you. We only use this information to reply to your inquiry.

Merchandise orders

We do not sell merchandise directly. All orders are placed through our Etsy shop, which collects your order, shipping, and payment details under its own privacy policy. As the seller, Etsy makes available to us your name, shipping address, the items you ordered, and any messages you send us through Etsy. We pass the name, shipping address, and item details to Printify, our print-on-demand partner, so it can produce and ship your order. We use this information only to fulfill your order, answer your questions about it, and keep the records that tax and accounting rules require. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Information collected automatically on our websites

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to understand general site usage (such as page views, referring sites, browser type, and approximate country). GoatCounter does not use tracking cookies and does not collect personally identifying information. See the GoatCounter privacy statement.

Game progress stored on your device

Laser Cobras! Assault stores your progress — high scores, upgrades, coins, unlocked cosmetic items, player profiles, and settings — locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, this same progress is mirrored to your own iCloud account so it can be backed up or carried to another device. That information stays on your device and within your personal Apple account. We do not operate servers that receive gameplay data, and we cannot see this information. The game has no account registration and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Game Center leaderboards (optional)

The game offers optional online leaderboards through Apple Game Center. Signing in is controlled entirely by your device and Apple ID — the game is fully playable without it and we never require it. When you are signed in, the game submits your numeric score to Apple's leaderboard service. Your Game Center nickname and score may then be displayed to other players inside Apple's own Game Center interface. We do not receive your name, email address, or Apple ID, we do not store Game Center information, and we do not combine it with anything else. Game Center is operated by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Cosmetic items in the game are sold as one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. Apple handles payment and tells the app which items you own. We never receive or store your payment details, card number, or billing address.

Advertising in our mobile game

Laser Cobras! Assault offers optional, opt-in rewarded video ads served through Google AdMob. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus, and you are never required to watch an ad to play or to progress. We serve only non-personalized ("non-targeted") ads, to every player, regardless of age or location. In plain terms:

  • What "non-targeted" means. Ads are chosen from the context of the moment — that you are playing our game, your device type and language, and an approximate (country- or city-level) location derived from your IP address. They are not chosen from a profile of you, your interests, or what you do in other apps or on other websites.
  • What we never do. We never ask for iOS tracking permission — because we do not track, there is nothing to ask for — so iOS gives the ad software no advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not let our advertising provider build an interest profile of you, and we do not share anything for behavioral advertising.
  • Ad content. We cap the ads Google may show in the game at its "parental guidance" (PG) content rating. That allows cartoon-style game ads but excludes all teen- and mature-rated ad content — such as alcohol, gambling, sexual content, weapons, and scary imagery — so that what appears is suitable for younger players who may be watching. Ads are made by third-party advertisers, not by us; if you ever see one you think is inappropriate, please tell us and we will report it to Google.
  • What Google still processes. To deliver, measure, and secure ads, Google and its advertising partners process limited technical information from your device: your IP address, device model and operating system, the app's identifier, language, the coarse location derived from your IP address, and basic ad-interaction data (for example, whether an ad played to completion). Google uses limited device-level signals only to support the game's operation — serving contextual ads, capping how often the same ad repeats, aggregate ad reporting, and detecting fraud and abuse — and not to contact you or build a profile of you. We do not receive this information ourselves.
  • If you tap an ad. If you tap an ad and install the advertised app, Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork may tell the advertiser that an install came from an ad shown in our game, without identifying you to them or to us.

For details, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

9. Children's privacy and who our game is for

Who Laser Cobras! Assault is for. We designed Laser Cobras! Assault for a general audience of teens and adults — quick arcade sessions for gamers. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not market it to children, and it is not listed in the App Store's Kids category. Under our Terms of Use, you must be at least 13 to use the game, and players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission.

We also want to be candid: the game's bright cartoon style, animated cobras, and cosmetic items may appeal to children younger than 13, and the game has no way to verify a player's age. So we built it as if a child might be playing, and the following protections apply to every player — not only to players we know to be children:

  • No accounts, no personal information. The game never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photo, or location, and has no sign-up.
  • No chat, messaging, or user-generated content. There is no way for players to contact one another through the game.
  • Only non-personalized ads, capped at a parental-guidance content rating (Section 2). We never request tracking permission or the advertising identifier, and we do not permit our advertising provider to profile players or target ads by interest.
  • Nothing leaves the device except an optional Game Center score submitted to Apple, the technical data the ad software needs to show an ad, and Apple's own processing of purchases.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Parents can require approval for every purchase with Apple's Ask to Buy, and can restrict in-app purchases and Game Center with Screen Time.

Persistent identifiers and COPPA. The game does not collect personal information from children as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), except to the limited extent that our advertising provider, Google AdMob, processes an IP address and device-level signals — which COPPA treats as "persistent identifiers" — solely to support the game's internal operations: serving contextual (non-personalized) ads, capping how often an ad repeats, aggregate ad measurement, and detecting fraud and abuse. We ensure those identifiers are not used to contact anyone or for behavioral advertising by (1) configuring AdMob to request only non-personalized ads on every ad request, (2) never requesting the device's advertising identifier or tracking permission, so iOS supplies the ad software with no advertising identifier, (3) integrating no other advertising or analytics software in the game, and (4) declaring "no tracking" in the game's Apple privacy manifest and App Store privacy label.

Parents and guardians. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us — for example, through our contact form — please contact us or email privacy@cobraverse.net. We will delete it promptly, and you may refuse any further collection or use. Progress stored by the game lives only on the device and in the family's iCloud account; you can remove it by deleting the app or by managing iCloud data in Settings.

Teens and U.S. state privacy laws. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use anyone's personal information — including that of consumers under 16 or under 18 — for targeted advertising or profiling, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act, the Colorado Privacy Act, and similar state laws.
