Privacy Policy

Draft — Last updated: August 20, 2026

DRAFT — not yet in effect. This policy is a working draft published ahead of the release of our mobile game Laser Cobras! Assault and is pending review by legal counsel. It describes our intended practices and may change before launch.

This Privacy Policy explains how The Cobraverse Company LLC ("Cobraverse," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit cobraverse.net, lasercobras.com, and our related sites and services (the "Services"), including any mobile applications we publish. By using the Services, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

The Cobraverse Company LLC is a media studio that produces video games, videos, and merchandise. You can contact us any time through our contact form.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide so we can respond to you. We only use this information to reply to your inquiry.

Merchandise orders

We do not sell merchandise directly. All orders are placed through our Etsy shop, which collects your order, shipping, and payment details under its own privacy policy. Etsy makes the order and shipping information needed to produce and deliver your item available to us as the seller and to Printify, our print-on-demand fulfillment partner. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Information collected automatically on our websites

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to understand general site usage (such as page views, referring sites, browser type, and approximate country). GoatCounter does not use tracking cookies and does not collect personally identifying information. See the GoatCounter privacy statement.

Game progress stored on your device

Our mobile game Laser Cobras! Assault stores your progress — high scores, upgrades, coins, unlocked cosmetic items, player profiles, and settings — locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, this same progress is mirrored to your own iCloud account so it can be backed up or carried to another device. That information stays on your device and within your personal Apple account. We do not operate servers that receive gameplay data, and we cannot see this information.

Game Center leaderboards (optional)

The game offers optional online leaderboards through Apple Game Center. Signing in is controlled entirely by your device and Apple ID — the game is fully playable without it and we never require it. When you are signed in, the game submits your numeric score to Apple's leaderboard service. Your Game Center nickname and score may then be displayed to other players inside Apple's own Game Center interface. We do not receive your name, email address, or Apple ID, we do not store Game Center information, and we do not combine it with anything else. Game Center is operated by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Cosmetic items in the game are sold as one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. Apple handles payment and tells the app which items you own. We never receive or store your payment details, card number, or billing address.

Advertising in our mobile game

Laser Cobras! Assault offers optional, opt-in rewarded video ads served through Google AdMob — you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus, and you are never required to watch an ad to play. We configure AdMob to serve only non-personalized ads: no advertising identifier is used to build a profile of you or to target ads based on your interests, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. To serve and measure these ads, Google and its partners may still process limited information from your device, such as IP address, device and app information, and general interaction data, along with a non-identifying signal used to limit how often the same ad is shown. We do not receive this information ourselves. For details, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Third-party service providers

We rely on a small number of service providers to operate the Services:

  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact-form submissions to our email. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.
  • Etsy — our merchandise is sold through our Etsy shop (etsy.com/shop/TheCobraverseCompany). If you visit our shop or make a purchase, Etsy independently collects and processes your information (including order and payment details) under its own privacy policy, over which we have no control. See the Etsy Privacy Policy.
  • Printify — print-on-demand fulfillment. Receives the order and shipping information needed to produce and ship your merchandise. See the Printify Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts our websites and may process standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver the site.
  • YouTube — some pages may embed videos; YouTube may set cookies and collect data under Google's policies.
  • Google AdMob — serves the optional rewarded ads in our mobile game Laser Cobras! Assault, configured for non-personalized delivery only. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google's ad technology overview.
  • Apple — operates the App Store, in-app purchases, Game Center leaderboards, and iCloud backup of your game progress. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and advertising identifiers

Our websites do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Some third-party features above (for example, embedded YouTube videos, or Etsy if you visit our shop) may set their own cookies when you interact with them, governed by their respective policies.

Our mobile game Laser Cobras! Assault uses Google AdMob configured to serve only non-personalized ads, which do not rely on a persistent advertising identifier to profile or target users. Because the game does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, it does not ask for tracking permission. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent message before ads are shown. Because serving ads — even non-personalized ones — involves storing and accessing information on your device, we ask for your consent (or, where applicable, rely on another lawful basis) before doing so, as required by the ePrivacy rules and the GDPR. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the game's settings.

5. How we use information

  • To respond to your messages and provide customer support.
  • To fulfill and deliver merchandise orders.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent misuse or abuse.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with the service providers listed above as needed to operate the Services, or when required by law. Our advertising provider (Google AdMob) independently processes limited device and usage information through our mobile game to serve and measure non-personalized ads, as described in Sections 2 and 4.

7. Data retention

  • Contact-form messages — kept as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our records, then deleted when no longer required.
  • Merchandise order information — retained by Etsy and Printify under their own retention policies; we do not maintain a separate copy of payment details.
  • Website analytics — GoatCounter retains only aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics.
  • Game progress — stays on your device and in your own iCloud account for as long as you keep the app installed. Deleting the app removes the local copy; iCloud-stored data can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.
  • Game Center scores — retained by Apple under Apple's policies for as long as the leaderboards exist.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information we hold, and our websites are served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Laser Cobras! Assault is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The game has no account registration and does not ask for or collect a name, email address, phone number, or physical address, and it has no chat, messaging, or other user-generated content features.

Regardless of a player's age, we serve only non-personalized ads throughout the game. We do not permit our advertising provider to build behavioral profiles of players or to target ads based on interests, and we do not track players across other companies' apps or websites.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the game or our websites. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. Apple's Screen Time and Family Sharing controls also let parents manage a child's Game Center participation and in-app purchases at the device level.

10. International users and data transfers

We are based in the United States. The service providers listed in Section 3 may process information in the United States and in other countries. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data-protection laws differ from those of your own. Where required, our providers rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us — for example, through our contact form or a merchandise order. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, or the Colorado Privacy Act. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising as those terms are defined under those laws, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Game progress stored on your device is under your control: deleting the app removes the local copy, and iCloud-stored progress can be managed from your device's iCloud settings.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this Policy? Reach us through our contact form.
